# Growth playbook — @romchekcrypto

Read this before making ANY post (morning or evening). Apply every rule in "Current rules".

moves proven ideas into "Current rules" and drops what didn't work.

## Baseline (2026-11-18, Metricool, posts 2026-10-10 … 2026-11-15)
- Followers: 48 → 70 in 5 weeks. New follows attributed to posts: 0.
- Avg reach per carousel ≈ 15 people (≈ 20% of followers) → posts barely reach non-followers.
- Reach trend: ~19 per post in mid-October → ~14 in November (falling).
- Saves and shares almost zero (≈ 4 saves and 1 share over ~70 posts). Likes 0–3.
- The few Reels (Nov 9–13) got 33–86 views each, i.e. more than carousels' reach.
- Best posting hours (Metricool, audience activity, MSK): peak 10:00–12:00, second peak 17:00–18:00;
  19:00 is already lower than 18:00. Weekends are ~2× weaker than weekdays.
- Diagnosis: Instagram shows carousels mostly to followers; growth to new people comes from
  saves/shares/sends (signals) and from Reels. Our posts generate almost no such signals.

## Current rules
1. **Hashtags: max 5** (Instagram limit since late 2025; extra ones hurt). Always #биткоин #обзорбиткоина
   #альтсезон + 2 by topic.
2. **Cover = hook, not a title.** 3–7 words, a concrete number or conflict, a curiosity gap
   ("Почему биткоин не растёт, хотя Nvidia взлетела?", "90% трейдеров ставят стоп сюда — и теряют").
   No jargon a beginner won't understand on the cover; jargon goes on slide 2.
3. **Slide 2 pays off the cover immediately** (the answer in one line), then details.
   People who don't get value by slide 2 leave — and swipe-through rate drives distribution.
4. **Less text, bigger text.** Max ~35 words per middle slide; every slide must read on a phone
   without zooming.
5. **Every post asks for a signal**, specific to the topic, in the caption AND on the cheat-sheet slide:
   - 💾 save ("Сохрани, чтобы…" + concrete reason),
   - 📤 send ("Отправь другу, который…" — names a person type),
   - 💬 an easy comment question (choice A/B, a number, a coin) — not an open essay.
6. **Caption line 1 = keywords people search** (Instagram search indexes captions):
   "биткоин прогноз", "биткоин новости", "как торговать", the indicator's name, etc.
7. Morning digest must say *what it means for the reader today* (levels + scenarios), not only news.
8. Evening education: one idea per post, practical ("как найти / где вход / где стоп"), schematic charts.
