# Growth playbook — @romchekcrypto

Read this before making ANY post (morning or evening). Apply every rule in "Current rules".
The weekly analysis task updates this file: it adds a dated entry to "Weekly log",
moves proven ideas into "Current rules" and drops what didn't work.

## GOAL (owner, 2026-11-18)
**≈100 new followers per week**, then move them to the Telegram channel. Until the goal is reached, every weekly
review must change something (content, format, rules) — never "keep as is".
KPIs tracked weekly: followers gained (IGEV43) − lost (IGEV44); follows per post (IGPO29); reach per post;
sends/shares per reach (IGPO27 ÷ IGPO14) — the #1 signal for non-follower reach; saves per reach.

## How Instagram recommends (checked 2026-11-18)
- Ranking is per-reach: **sends per reach** (strongest for non-followers), **watch time / swipe-through**,
  likes per reach (stronger for followers). Raw totals don't matter, rates do.
- Only original content is recommended; aggregators and reposts are excluded. Our own designs are fine.
  Reels must have no watermarks and no borders, 9:16, hook in the first 3 seconds, ≤3 min.
- Reels reach ~2× more non-followers than other formats; carousels get ~2× more engagement per person reached.
  → Reels bring new people, carousels convert and get saved. We need BOTH.
- A skipped carousel can be shown again starting from slide 2 → slide 2 must work as a second cover.
- Trial Reels (shown only to non-followers) need ≥1,000 followers — not available to us yet.
- Search: keywords in profile name, bio and the first caption line make the account findable.

## Baseline (2026-11-18, Metricool, posts 2026-10-10 … 2026-11-15)
- Followers: 48 → 70 in 5 weeks. New follows attributed to posts: 0.
- Avg reach per carousel ≈ 15 people (≈ 20% of followers) → posts barely reach non-followers.
- Reach trend: ~19 per post in mid-October → ~14 in November (falling).
- Saves and shares almost zero (≈ 4 saves and 1 share over ~70 posts). Likes 0–3.
- The few Reels (Nov 9–13) got 33–86 views each, i.e. more than carousels' reach.
- Best posting hours (Metricool, audience activity, MSK): peak 10:00–12:00, second peak 17:00–18:00;
  19:00 is already lower than 18:00. Weekends are ~2× weaker than weekdays.
- Diagnosis: Instagram shows carousels mostly to followers; growth to new people comes from
  saves/shares/sends (signals) and from Reels. Our posts generate almost no such signals.

## Current rules
1. **Hashtags: max 5** (Instagram limit since late 2025; extra ones hurt). Always #биткоин #обзорбиткоина
   #альтсезон + 2 by topic.
2. **Cover = the reader's PAIN or trigger, never a term.** (Owner, 2026-11-18: «ИМБАЛАНС / FVG» has no trigger,
   nobody wants to read it.) Describe a painful situation the reader has lived: «Купил на импульсе, а цена вниз?»,
   «Стоп выбило — и цена пошла в твою сторону?», «Ликвидировали на шпильке?». The term goes to slide 2.
   **SHORT: 2 lines, 2–4 words total, huge type** (owner: long headline is bad) — e.g. «КУПИЛ / НА ХАЯХ?» + one
   short subline. Check the cover is centred and nothing overflows (wide nowrap lines need negative side margins).
   **Cover = hook, not a title.** 3–7 words, a concrete number or conflict, a curiosity gap
   ("Почему биткоин не растёт, хотя Nvidia взлетела?", "90% трейдеров ставят стоп сюда — и теряют").
   No jargon a beginner won't understand on the cover; jargon goes on slide 2.
3. **Slide 2 pays off the cover immediately** (the answer in one line), then details.
   People who don't get value by slide 2 leave — and swipe-through rate drives distribution.
4. **Less text, bigger text.** Max ~35 words per middle slide; every slide must read on a phone
   without zooming.
5. **Every post asks for a signal**, specific to the topic, in the caption AND on the cheat-sheet slide:
   - 💾 save ("Сохрани, чтобы…" + concrete reason),
   - 📤 send ("Отправь другу, который…" — names a person type),
   - 💬 an easy comment question (choice A/B, a number, a coin) — not an open essay.
6. **Caption line 1 = keywords people search** (Instagram search indexes captions):
   "биткоин прогноз", "биткоин новости", "как торговать", the indicator's name, etc.
7. Morning digest must say *what it means for the reader today* (levels + scenarios), not only news.
8. Evening education: one idea per post, practical ("как найти / где вход / где стоп"), schematic charts.
9a. **Owner's reference covers (2026-11-18, @therea.ai screenshots) — copy the PRINCIPLES, never the text:**
   - The headline is a complete thought you want to finish reading, not a term: paradox («Дейтрейдинг — единственная
     работа, где самое сложное — ничего не делать»), concrete practical promise («Стратегия на 4-часовой свече» +
     subline «Простая модель для внутридневной торговли» — most SENDS, 24), ratio («80% психология / 20% стратегия»),
     pain («Что делать после крупного убытка»), list + curiosity («5 фаз рынка, о которых ты не знал»).
   - ONE accent phrase only (the emotional or concrete part), everything else white.
   - Minimalism: dark background, lots of empty space, ONE simple big visual (2 candles, one chart, a "?" , a person).
     No clutter, no small text on the cover except one subline.
   - APPROVED by the owner (2026-11-18): posts/2026-11-18-1800-fvg-trade-setup-btc/slide_1.jpg — «СТРАТЕГИЯ ВХОДА
     ПОСЛЕ ИМПУЛЬСА» + subline, left-aligned 128px uppercase, one lime phrase, one big simple diagram, no tag pill.
     Template: tools/cover_minimal.html (replace /*T*/, /*SUB*/, positions; draw a correct schematic for the topic).
   - Practical "save & try it" posts (a strategy / model / checklist) get the most sends → do them often.
9. **Evening covers use proven hook formulas** (from @therea.ai, see below), rotate them:
   - stat claim: "90% трейдеров пропускают лучший вход", "80% психология — 20% стратегия";
   - numbered list: "5 фаз рынка, о которых ты не знал", "5 индикаторов для новичка";
   - first person: "Как я ищу сделки на 1ч и 5м", "5 правил, которые я не нарушаю" (never invent experience,
     years or results about Ромчик — only general first-person framing);
   - pain / emotion: "Что делать после крупного убытка";
   - time promise: "Разберём за 60 секунд".
   The cover tells the BENEFIT or the PAIN, not the indicator's name ("RSI" alone is a weak cover).
10. **Write in Ромчик's first-person voice** ("я", "мой стоп", "как я делаю") — personality builds trust;
    neutral textbook tone is what everyone else does.
11. **Psychology, mistakes and stories perform** — at least 2 evening posts a week should be about trader psychology,
    typical mistakes, or a crypto story (e.g. "CZ продал квартиру ради биткоина в 2014") instead of a pure indicator lesson.

12. **Slide 2 is a second cover**: its headline must hook on its own (Instagram re-serves carousels from slide 2).
13. **Ask to FOLLOW, not only to join Telegram.** Cheat-sheet slide and caption get a follow line with a reason:
    "Подпишись — каждый день в 10:00 разбор рынка, в 18:00 урок трейдинга". Telegram CTA stays on the last slide.
14. **Recurring rubrics** give a reason to follow: evening posts are numbered "Урок #N" (continue the count in the
    weekly log), morning posts keep a constant "Крипто-утро" tag.
15. **Designed for sending**: every post has one line/slide that is natural to forward
    ("Отправь тому, кто торгует с плечом x50") — a checklist, a mistake, a myth vs fact.

## Titles, captions, hashtags, keywords — Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (researched 2026-11-18; owner: «не борщи с хэштегами, не повторяй весь текст ролика»)
Facts:
- Instagram: max 5 hashtags per post/Reel since 2025-12-19; hashtags only help categorise, they barely affect reach (Mosseri).
  Keywords in the caption (esp. the first ~125 visible chars), on-screen text and spoken words are the main relevance/search
  signals. Display name and bio are searched too. Generic tags (#reels #explore #viral) can hurt.
- YouTube (Help Center): title, thumbnail and description matter most; «tags play a minimal role» (useful mainly for
  misspellings); up to 3 hashtags from the description are shown above the title; >60 hashtags → all ignored; #shorts is
  not required. YouTube auto-transcribes the voice, so the spoken keyword counts.
- Competitors (YouTube search «трейдинг ликвидность», 2026-11-18): the best Shorts (31k, 19k, 10k, 5k views) start the
  title with the search keyword + a pain/question («Что такое ликвидность в трейдинге и почему её постоянно собирают?»,
  «Ликвидность в трейдинге: как маркетмейкер забирает твои стопы»); weak ones are vague («Ликвидность двигает рынок»)
  or stuffed with 5+ hashtags/«#short #шортс».
Rules (apply every time):
1. ONE primary search keyword per video (e.g. «ликвидность в трейдинге», «стоп лосс», «ордер блок», «RSI»), 1–2 secondary.
   It must appear: in the first words of the title/first caption line, on screen in the first seconds, and in the voice.
2. Instagram caption: line 1 = keyword + hook in ≤ 110 chars (e.g. «🎯 Ликвидность в трейдинге: почему выбивает твой стоп»);
   then 1–2 SHORT lines with the key takeaway (NOT the voice script retold); then CTAs (save / send to a friend /
   «обучение»), subscribe line, Telegram line, disclaimer. Whole caption ≤ ~600 chars.
3. Instagram hashtags: 3–5 total, specific: #биткоин #обзорбиткоина #альтсезон (owner's standing set) + 1–2 exact topic
   tags (#ликвидность, #стоплосс, #смартмани…). Never generic ones.
4. YouTube title: 40–70 chars, keyword first, then the pain/question; at most one CAPS word; no hashtags in the title
   (they go in the description); no clickbait the video doesn't answer.
5. YouTube description: first sentence = keyword + what the viewer learns (2–3 sentences total, no full script), CTA
   «обучение», Telegram/Instagram lines, disclaimer, then 3 hashtags (#shorts #трейдинг + 1 topic, or #биткоин when the
   video is about BTC) — 3 is enough, they show above the title.
6. YouTube tags: owner wants the field filled (keep the 500-char rule), but they barely matter — never spend time on them
   instead of the title/hook, and never add unrelated tags.
7. Review in the weekly analysis: compare views/search traffic of videos with different title patterns and adjust these rules.
